Red palm weevil · 100 × 50 date palms at 8 m · one frame per day

Four years after a 2 × 2 introduction

The same four palms are infested on 1 January of year 1 in both films. Left, dead palms are replanted, on average 200 days after they die (the model default); right, they are not. Both films run from day 1 to day 1460 on the whole orchard, without a break, and play in step.

At 1× a year takes about 15 s (24 days a second). Space plays or pauses; the arrow keys step a day, Shift + arrow a week.

What each frame shows

  • Palms: white susceptible, amber cryptic (infested, no symptoms yet), red symptomatic, black dead.
  • Blue cloud: females flying over the last 7 days, log scale 0.01–10 per palm.
  • Navy outline: a palm releasing at least one flying female that day.
  • Rings: black, infested that day; grey and fading, infested in the previous 6 days.
  • Lines: likely source of each infestation in the last 7 days (top source weight ≥ 0.5; a reconstruction, not a record).
  • Strip: the whole orchard's females flying and new infestations per week for the current year, red cursor at the day.

Palms die on average 9.3 months after infestation (median 8.7, 10–90 % 5–14 months): about 2 months to symptoms, then a collapse spread over four hidden stages. Fewer than 0.2 % die within 3 months. The first black palm appears on 11 July of year 1 in the left film: the fastest of the four introduced palms.
